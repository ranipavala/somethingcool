Meet our Team

We are Vibe Divas — a dynamic team of four passionate individuals brought together by creativity, dedication, and a shared love for music and design. United under the vibrant leadership of Khirty, our team consists of Pavala, Chua, and Ethar, each bringing their unique strengths to the stage.

Empowered by Collaboration

We believe in the power of teamwork and the magic that happens when ideas flow freely. Every decision we make is a reflection of our shared vision.

Creating for a Cause

From conceptualizing the theme to building this site, we’ve poured our hearts into making this festival experience unforgettable — not just for us, but for every visitor.
